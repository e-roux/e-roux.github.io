## Dataflow programming

- programming paradigm that models a program as a directed graph of the data flowing between operations.
- share some features of functional languages, more suitable for numeric processing.
- analogy to series of workers on an assembly line.
- operations are only concerned with the availability of data inputs.
- pioneered by Jack Dennis at MIT in the 1960s.

## Directed Acyclic Graph

- contains directed edges
- no loops or cycles: no circular dependencies, no deadlock
- identification of tasks to be run in parallel
- separation of problems in incremental tasks
